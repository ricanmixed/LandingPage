A Higher Standard for Sober Living

At **Patriot House Sober Living**, we recognize that sustainable recovery requires more than just a place to sleep. It requires an environment rooted in purpose, mutual respect, discipline, and authentic brotherhood.

Founded with a mission to serve military veterans, emergency responders, and dedicated individuals, our residences foster clean living while equipping residents with actionable life skills, employment resources, and peer support.

Designed for Total Recovery Success

24/7 House Supervision

Random Drug Testing

Community Guidelines

Clear Rules build Strong Character

Structure is the cornerstone of successful sober living. Our guidelines are straightforward, fair, and rigorously enforced to maintain safety and peace for all residents.

Zero Tolerance Policy: Any possession or use of alcohol or illicit substances results in immediate discharge and referral to detoxification services.

100% Sobriety

Complete abstinence from drugs and alcohol. Attendance at mandatory urinalysis screens.

Curfew Adherence

Standard curfew is 10:00 PM weekdays / 11:00 PM weekends for developing healthy routines.

12-Step Meetings

Must attend at least 4 recovery meetings per week and maintain active contact with a sponsor.

Employment / Purpose

Must be gainfully employed, actively job hunting, attending school, or performing community service.

Daily House Chores

Residents maintain personal room cleanliness and participate in assigned communal duties.

House Meetings

Mandatory weekly resident community meetings to review goals, resolve conflicts, and plan meals.
